About

Bonnie Sanchez MA, RDN, LDN, CDCES

I am a registered dietitian nutritionist, certified diabetes care and education specialist. I became a dietitian because I love food and love helping people! I have personally experienced the challenges of trying to make healthy changes for myself and my family, and I know how overwhelming nutrition advice can feel.

My approach is practical, compassionate, and focused on realistic habits that fit your everyday life. Together, we will create a plan that supports your health goals while still leaving room for the foods and routines you enjoy.
